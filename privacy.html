Noodle Pets Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Your pets live only on your device. We don’t collect anything about you or your child. No ads, no tracking.

Who we are

Noodle Pets is a game for children made by up-skn (“we”, “us”). It is available on Apple’s App Store and, later, on Google Play. If you have any question about privacy, email us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. We answer within 30 days.

This policy covers the Noodle Pets app on iPhone, iPad and Android devices, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you or your child. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we never ask for a name, email, phone number, address, photo, voice or location.

What stays on the device

The game saves its progress only inside the app on the device: your pets and the names you give them, coins, stickers, drawings, clothes, settings, and the birth year picked at the start. The birth year is used only to fit the game to your child’s age. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools from other companies, we do not use the device’s advertising identifier (Apple’s IDFA or Google’s Advertising ID), and we do not track your child across other apps or websites. We do not sell or share any information.

Purchases

Coins, new friends and Noodle Pets Plus are optional and are bought through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Every purchase is behind a grown-up check. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your name or payment details. Their privacy policies apply to those purchases.

Device permissions

Photos: saving a picture asks your permission to add it to your photo library. The app only adds pictures and never reads your photos.

Motion sensor: pets can react when the device is tilted. This happens only on the device; nothing is saved or sent.

Reminders: if reminders are turned on in Settings, they are scheduled on the device only. You can turn them off at any time.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Sharing and ratings

Sharing a picture and rating the app are behind a grown-up check and use the device’s own share and rating screens. You choose where a picture goes; we receive nothing. The app has no chat and no way for children to talk to other people.

Children’s privacy

Noodle Pets is made for children. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing that needs parental consent. We follow the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, Apple’s rules for the Kids category and Google Play’s Families policy. Anything that leaves the game, like purchases, links, sharing or rating, is behind a grown-up check.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep no data about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to delete and no account to close. To delete everything the game saved, delete the app from the device. You can also email us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will confirm that we hold nothing.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or ads, and has no forms. Like any website, the server that hosts it and the service that provides its fonts (Google Fonts) receive your IP address to deliver the page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold, to correct it or to delete it. Because we hold none, the answer will be that there is nothing to share or delete. Parents and guardians can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with any question or request.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect. If we ever want to collect personal information, we will ask a parent first.
