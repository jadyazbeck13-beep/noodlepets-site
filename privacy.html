Noodle Pets Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Your pets live only on your device. We don’t collect anything about you or your child. No ads, no tracking.

Who we are

Noodle Pets is made by Jad Yazbeck. If you have any question about privacy, email us at hello@noodlepets.app. We answer within 30 days.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you or your child. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no ads, and we never ask for a name, email, phone number, address, photo, birthday or location. The microphone and photo features below work only on the device.

What stays on this device

The game saves its progress only inside the app on this device: your pets and the names you give them, coins, stickers, drawings, clothes, game levels and scores, and settings. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it from the device (a copy can stay in your own iCloud or Google device backup, which you control).

Leaderboards in the mini games

Some mini games keep a list of the best scores. When a child gets a top score, they can type a name next to it. These lists stay on this device only: they are never sent online, never shared with other players and never seen by us. We suggest using a first name or a nickname. Deleting the app deletes the lists.

Microphone

When your child taps the talk button, the app asks to use the microphone. It listens only while the talk button is held (at most 8 seconds) so the pet can answer back in a funny voice. The recording stays on the device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted right after it plays. You can turn the microphone off at any time in your device settings.

Motion, sound and vibration

The game can use the device’s motion sensor so pets react when the device is tilted, it plays sounds, and it can give a soft vibration when buttons are tapped (you can turn vibration off in Settings). This happens only on the device and nothing is saved or sent.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools from other companies, we do not use the advertising identifier, and we do not track your child across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Coins, new friends and Noodle Pets Plus are bought through Apple’s App Store with real money. Every purchase is behind a grown-up check. Apple handles the payment and the receipt; we never see your name or payment details. Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply to those purchases.

Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple’s refund rules; we cannot issue refunds ourselves. To stop purchases you did not approve, we recommend turning on Ask to Buy or Screen Time purchase limits on your child’s device.

Coins and game items

Coins, unlocked games, clothes and other items in the game are virtual items for use only inside Noodle Pets. They have no value in real money, cannot be exchanged for cash or anything outside the game, cannot be sold or moved to another person, and are a licence to use them in the game, not something you own.

Because there are no accounts, coins, items and progress are saved only on this device. They can be lost if the app is deleted, the device is reset, lost or replaced, or the device’s storage is cleared, and we are not able to bring them back. Permanent purchases (new friends, Noodle Pets Plus and the starter pack’s game and crown) stay linked to your Apple account and come back with Restore in Settings.

To keep the game fair and fun, we may change how many coins things cost or give, change or remove games and items, or fix mistakes in a later update. Rewards gained by changing the device’s date or time, or by any other trick or error, may be removed.

Fair play and game rules

Noodle Pets follows the real clock. Pets get hungry, grow and earn rewards over real days, and coins come from caring for them when they really need it. To keep the game fair, the game has daily limits on how many coins can be earned, a pet needs a real sleep before a new day counts, and some rewards can only be won once.

Changing the device’s date, time or time zone to get rewards, using bugs or glitches, or using cheats, modified versions of the app or other tools to get coins, items, levels or scores faster is against these rules. When the game notices this, it can stop giving rewards for a while, and coins, items, progress, rewards or scores gained this way may be removed, reset or corrected, without a refund, as far as the law allows.

The daily limits, prices and how many coins things give may change in updates to keep the game fair and fun. Starting over keeps what was bought, but rewards that can only be won once are not given again.

Photos, sharing and ratings

Saving a picture asks your permission to add it to your Photos. The app only adds pictures and never looks at your photo library.

Sharing a picture and rating the app are behind a grown-up check and use your device’s own share and rating screens. You choose where a picture goes; we receive nothing.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules gentle care reminders on this device only (at most a few a day). They can show your pet’s name on the lock screen. You can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

Game updates

To get game fixes and new content without a new download, the app checks for updates when it opens, using the update service of Expo (our app platform). Like any internet request, this sends the device’s internet address and a random install number that is used only to deliver the right update. It is not used for ads, tracking or profiles, and it contains no name or other information about your child.

Children’s privacy

Noodle Pets is made for children. We do not collect personal information from children, so we do not ask for parental consent, and we follow the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. Anything that leaves the game, like purchases, sharing or rating, is behind a grown-up check.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep no data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. To delete everything the game saved, delete the app from the device.

Terms of use

Your use of Noodle Pets is also covered by Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA). The app is provided “as is”. We may update, change or stop any part of the game at any time. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for lost progress, coins or items, or for any indirect loss from using the app. Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under the law where you live.

Your rights

Parents and guardians can contact us at hello@noodlepets.app with any question or request about privacy, and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect. If we ever want to collect personal information, we will ask a parent first.
