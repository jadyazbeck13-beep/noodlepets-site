Noodle Pets Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Your pets live only on your device. We don’t collect anything about you or your child. No ads, no tracking.

Who we are

Noodle Pets is made by up-skn. If you have any question about privacy, email us at hello@noodlepets.app. We answer within 30 days.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you or your child. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and we never ask for a name, email, phone number, address, photo, voice or location.

What stays on this device

The game saves its progress only inside the app on this device: your pets and the names you give them, coins, stickers, drawings, clothes, settings, and the birth year picked at the start. The birth year is only used to fit the game to your child’s age. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools from other companies, we do not use the advertising identifier, and we do not track your child across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Coins, new friends and Noodle Pets Plus are bought through Apple’s App Store. Purchases are behind a grown-up check. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those purchases.

Photos, sharing and ratings

Saving a picture asks your permission to add it to your Photos. The app only adds pictures and never looks at your photo library.

Sharing a picture and rating the app are behind a grown-up check and use your device’s own share and rating screens. You choose where a picture goes; we receive nothing.

Motion and sound

The game can use the device’s motion sensor so pets react when the device is tilted, and it plays sounds. This happens only on the device and nothing is saved or sent.

Reminders

If reminders are turned on in Settings, they are scheduled on this device only. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.

Children’s privacy

Noodle Pets is made for children. Because we collect no personal information, we do not need to ask for parental consent, and we follow the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. Anything that leaves the game, like purchases, sharing or rating, is behind a grown-up check.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep no data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. To delete everything the game saved, delete the app from the device.

Your rights

Parents and guardians can contact us at hello@noodlepets.app with any question or request about privacy, and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect. If we ever want to collect personal information, we will ask a parent first.
