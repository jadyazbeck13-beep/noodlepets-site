You can also write to us for anything about privacy, purchases or your child's account.

I deleted the app and my pets are gone

Noodle Pets saves your game in your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the device, make sure iCloud Drive is on, then open the app: your pets, coins and items come back by themselves. If they do not, email us before starting again and we will help.

I bought something and it is missing

Open Settings inside the game and tap Restore Purchases. This brings back Noodle Pets Plus, friends you bought and the starter pack on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Coins are used up as you spend them, so they are not restored.

I want a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

How do I stop my child buying things?

Every purchase in Noodle Pets is already behind a grown-up check: a small sum you have to work out before the store opens. For an extra layer, turn on Ask to Buy for your child's account, or use Screen Time to block in-app purchases on the device.

How do I turn notifications off?

Open Settings inside the game. There are two switches. Care reminders tell you when your pet needs something. News and events is off unless a grown-up turns it on, and tells you about new pets and events. You can turn either one off at any time, or turn all of them off in your device's own Settings under Noodle Pets.

The microphone

When your child holds the talk button, the pet listens for a few seconds and repeats what it heard in a funny voice. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. If you prefer, simply do not allow the microphone: the rest of the game works normally.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app from the device. We hold no information about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove the iCloud copy as well, open your device Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, and delete the Noodle Pets data.

I found a bug, or I have an idea

Please tell us at hello@noodlepets.app. Say which device you use and what happened, and we will look into it.

Age and content

Noodle Pets is made for children aged 4 and up. There are no ads, no tracking, no chat and no links to other people's content. Anything that leaves the game is behind a grown-up check.
